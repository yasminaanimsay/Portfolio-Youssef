Overview

A building shaped like the hills around it.

Set in the heart of historic Tétouan, with the mountains as a backdrop, View Center rethinks the shopping centre. Its fluid, sinuous architecture echoes the curves of the surrounding hills and the movement of the sea: a biomimetic design in dialogue with its setting that keeps the character of Tétouan.

Inside, natural light wells fill the malls with soft light, while wood and stone give a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Gardens and pedestrian walks around the building invite residents to use it beyond shopping, as a place to meet and spend time.
