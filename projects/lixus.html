Lixus Renaissance

Renaissance of a historic landscape: a heritage park that unveils the Phoenician and Roman layers of Lixus.

Programme
Heritage & tourism park
Location
Lixus, Larache
Year
2025
Scales
Urban + architectural
01 03
Overview

Unveiling the past, as if lifting a veil.

The urban and architectural strategy for Lixus balances the preservation of the ancient site with reversible contemporary interventions. A narrative route begins at a welcome pavilion set into the topography, a hinge between Larache and the site that orients visitors.

At its heart, a site museum and the reactivated amphitheatre offer cultural immersion and entertainment, while light walkways and minimal shelters protect the ruins. The approach favours historical legibility and the emotion of the landscape, turning the ruins into a living place that strengthens the region’s appeal without distorting its Phoenician and Roman layers.

Concept
History, nature and contemporary life in one project
Goal
Give Lixus new value as a cultural and tourist destination
Theme
Reveal the past with forms drawn from the landscape
Audience
Residents, researchers and visitors
Site masterplan

A route that follows the topography.

The plan links a welcome centre, a cable car, a nautical club, leisure areas, a restaurant, lodging pavilions and the museum, each one a continuation of the landscape rather than an object placed on it. The archaeological perimeter is left to light walkways.

Strategy

Flows, functions and levels.
