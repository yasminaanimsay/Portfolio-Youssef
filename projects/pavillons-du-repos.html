Overview

Where the museum teaches, the pavilion invites you to stay.

The lodging pavilion is the natural extension of the Lixus urban project. Its role is clear: to let visitors extend their stay on the archaeological site beyond a single day, in a calm setting close to nature and suited to contemplation.

In the morning, guests wake up to an open view of the landscape. They can join the museum for research and discovery, or walk the ruins at their own pace. In the evening, they return to an intimate, peaceful space to rest and enjoy the silence of the place.
