Overview

Density that still feels like home.

This collective housing project in Tangier takes a contemporary approach to dwelling, reconciling urban density, residential comfort and quality living spaces. Through a functional organisation and close attention to daylight, ventilation and outdoor space, it offers a pleasant setting suited to residents’ needs.

Staggered blocks create a visual rhythm and open panoramic views for every building, while most flats get two facades. The generous green space becomes a garden with play areas and sports facilities, encouraging conviviality and living together.
