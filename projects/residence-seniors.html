Overview

A residence where growing older does not mean growing apart. Five modular volumes gather around vertical cores, wrapped in mashrabiya screens and timber-effect cladding, combining independent apartments with medical, social and leisure services under one roof.

Site & diagnosis

Reading the site before drawing a line.

The plot sits in Cabo Negro, in the north of Morocco. Two secondary roads bound it to the north and east, and two tertiary roads to the south and west. The diagnosis sorted the site's constraints into three families, and each one shaped a response.

ANatural constraints

Wadi crossing, soil conditions, climate exposure and construction difficulties.

BUrban constraints

Road layout, unused land, lack of street furniture, and a shortage of comfort and safety in public space.

CSocial constraints

Abrupt height differences, an 80% push for density, unemployment and an absence of social mix.

How do we steer the project through three pillars?

i.

Ecological

Pedestrian-first ground plane, compact massing and planted voids between volumes.

ii.

Environmental

Walls inclined by 10° to draw natural light deep into the units; screens that filter sun.

iii.

Social

A community-scaled ground floor of shared services that brings residents and neighbours together.

Masterplan · 1:500

A ground plane made for slow walking.

The housing bar runs along the edge of the plot and frees a sequence of landscaped courtyards, a pool and shaded paths. Clear 15 m gaps to the neighbouring blocks protect light and privacy.

Program

Homes above, life below.

The upper floors hold the apartments. The ground floor is a continuous base of administration and services, open to residents and the neighbourhood.

Design notes

Studio bedrooms have no direct window onto the street. Small terraces with angled glass balustrades light them instead.

In the T2, thick walls work as solid cores that hold the service zones. At the entrance they hide the kitchen.

In the T3, a terrace beside the kitchen doubles as a laundry area.
